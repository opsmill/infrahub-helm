When `upgrade.enabled` is true and the Infrahub image is 1.13 or later, the upgrade hook job runs `infrahub upgrade --no-task-history-cleanup`, followed by the arguments of `upgrade.extraArgs`. Helm waits at most 5 minutes for a hook by default, and the cleanup of a large task history can take longer. To clean up the task history, run `infrahub tasks flush flow-runs --rewrite` after the upgrade. With an image tag earlier than 1.13, the job does not pass the option, because `infrahub upgrade` does not have it.
